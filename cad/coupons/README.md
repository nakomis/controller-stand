# Fit-test coupons (CONT-3)

Generated by `tools/make_coupons.py` from `cad/reference-sections.csv`. Print these
before the full stand, and hold them against a real controller.

| File | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `centre-slice.stl` | The seat at the connector: front lip, valley, back-rest angle |
| `comb.stl` | The whole rest: ribs at x = 0, ±44, ±54 on a 2 mm plate. The controller should sit on it as on the full stand |

## Printing (resin, Cthulhu)

Both go on one Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra plate (`slice-3d` skill), **straight on the
(flexible) build plate with no supports**: comb base down, centre slice standing on its
flat bottom edge. Nothing overhangs, so the rest-surface edges come out unmarked, and the
comb's base sits on the same datum the rib heights are measured from. The first print
(24 Oct 2026, beige ABS-like at 2.0 s, `known-good` profile) was 1,682 layers, 84.1 mm,
about 2 h 8 min and ~42 ml, as `/local/ndfm-controller-stand-test-pieces-flat.goo`.
Flex the plate along the long edge to release the comb's base.

## What to look for

- **Centre slice:** with the controller's bottom edge in the valley, does the face lie
  flat against the back rest, or rock? Is there a gap at the lip?
- **Comb:** does it sit level, without rocking side to side? Do the grips touch the outer
  ribs, or hover?
- Anything off: note roughly how much (a feeler gauge or a stack of paper does) and at
  which rib, in `cad/measurements.md`.

## Looser variants

`--clearance` lowers every profile, e.g.
`python3 tools/make_coupons.py cad/reference-sections.csv cad/coupons/ --clearance 0.3`
writes `*-c0.3.stl`.
